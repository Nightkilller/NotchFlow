Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 • NotchFlow Open Source Project

This Privacy Policy explains how NotchFlow (“we”, “our”, or “the project”) handles information across both the NotchFlow macOS Application and the NotchFlow Website (https://notchflow.vercel.app/).

1. The NotchFlow macOS Application

We believe your presentation materials, notes, and spoken words are entirely private. The NotchFlow macOS desktop application operates under a strict on-device computing architecture:

  • Zero Audio Transmission: Microphone input captured during teleprompter sessions is processed exclusively on your Mac via Apple's built-in SFSpeechRecognizer. Audio streams are never recorded to disk, uploaded to cloud servers, or transmitted across any network.
  • Local Script Storage: Presentation scripts imported into NotchFlow remain stored locally on your device within your standard user application directory.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The desktop application contains no tracking pixels, telemetry probes, user tracking code, or background crash reporting services.
  • Offline Capability: NotchFlow functions completely without an active internet connection.

2. The NotchFlow Website

When you visit our informational website (https://notchflow.vercel.app/), certain standard web services process visitor data:

A. Hosting & Server Logs

The website is hosted on Vercel Inc. Vercel's edge servers automatically collect standard web server access logs, which include IP addresses, browser user agent strings, referring URLs, and request timestamps to maintain network security and content delivery performance.

B. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Our website uses Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-4982175209260588) to display relevant advertisements on informational pages. Google and third-party advertising vendors use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website and other sites across the internet:

  • Google uses the DoubleClick cookie and advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other websites.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

C. Email Waitlist & Contact Forms

When you voluntarily submit your email address via our waitlist subscription form or contact form, the data is transmitted securely to our email processing service (FormSubmit) and routed directly to our project mailbox (gutaadi@gmail.com). We use this information solely to send release notifications and respond to inquiries. We never sell, rent, or trade your contact information.

3. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external services such as GitHub (where source code and issue tracking reside) and Apple documentation. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

4. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location (such as under the GDPR in the European Economic Area or CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to or deletion of your email address from our subscriber records. To exercise these rights, please contact us at gutaadi@gmail.com.
