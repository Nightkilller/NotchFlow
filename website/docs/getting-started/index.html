Getting Started with NotchFlow

A quick introduction to presenting with voice-synchronized teleprompting on your Mac.

Prerequisites

Before running NotchFlow, ensure your Mac meets the following requirements:

  • An Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4 series chip).
  • Running macOS 14.0 Sonoma, macOS 15.0 Sequoia, or later.
  • A working microphone (internal MacBook mic, USB mic, or audio interface).

Step 1: Download & Open NotchFlow

Download the latest release disk image from our homepage or GitHub Releases. Open NotchFlow-Installer.dmg and drag the NotchFlow icon into your Applications folder.

Step 2: Grant Microphone Permission

When you first launch NotchFlow, macOS will present a system dialog requesting permission to access your microphone. Click OK. This permission is necessary for on-device voice recognition to track your speech pace. All audio analysis occurs strictly on your Mac's Neural Engine—no audio is ever recorded or uploaded.

Step 3: Load Your Script

Upon opening, NotchFlow presents the Editor window. You have two options:

  1. Type or Paste: Directly write your script in the clean Markdown editor area.
  2. Import File: Click Import Document... to load any Plain Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), or PDF (.pdf) file. NotchFlow automatically sanitizes line wraps and formatting markers.

Step 4: Launch the Notch Prompter

Click Start Teleprompter. The window will smoothly anchor itself directly underneath your MacBook's camera notch (or top-center on external monitors).

Step 5: Present Naturally

Begin speaking your script aloud. As you speak:

  • The active word highlights in signature high-contrast orange.
  • Completed words dim softly to reduce visual noise.
  • Upcoming text advances smoothly at your natural speaking cadence.

Move your mouse away to let NotchFlow collapse into compact 2-line glance mode. Hover over it whenever you need to adjust speed, font size, or reset your position.
