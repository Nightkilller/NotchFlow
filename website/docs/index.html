NotchFlow Documentation

Complete technical documentation, setup instructions, hardware requirements, and troubleshooting guides for NotchFlow on macOS.

NotchFlow is a native macOS teleprompter designed specifically for Apple Silicon MacBooks. It anchors directly below the display camera notch to help you maintain direct eye contact with your audience while tracking your spoken words in real time.
