Troubleshooting Guide

Diagnostic steps and solutions for common audio, permission, window, and speech synchronization issues.

Issue 1: Teleprompter Not Following Voice

Symptom: You begin speaking, but the highlighted word does not advance.

  1. Verify Microphone Permissions: Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. Confirm that the toggle switch next to NotchFlow is enabled.
  2. Check Active Input Device: Open System Settings > Sound > Input. Verify that your desired microphone is selected and that the Input Level meter moves when you speak.
  3. Inspect Background Noise: High ambient noise (such as loud fans or music) can lower speech recognition confidence. Move closer to your microphone or use a headset mic.
  4. Check Debug HUD: Press D while presenting to display the real-time recognition confidence score and buffer window.

Issue 2: Notch Alignment Off-Center on External Monitor

Symptom: The prompter pod appears displaced when using an external display.

  • NotchFlow queries the display where the cursor currently resides. Move your mouse to the external display before clicking Start Teleprompter.
  • On notchless external screens, NotchFlow anchors to the top center. You can freely drag the window to reposition it anywhere along your display bezel.

Issue 3: “App is damaged and can't be opened” Alert

Symptom: macOS Gatekeeper blocks opening newly downloaded DMG builds.

This occurs when quarantine attributes are applied by the browser to open-source software without an expensive commercial developer certificate. To clear the quarantine attribute: 

xattr -cr /Applications/NotchFlow.app

Then open NotchFlow normally from your Applications folder.

Issue 4: Resetting App Preferences

If you ever wish to restore NotchFlow to its factory default window sizes, themes, and settings: 

defaults delete com.adityagupta.notchflow

Still Need Assistance?

If your issue persists, please submit an issue on our GitHub Issues tracker or email us at gutaadi@gmail.com with your macOS version and Mac model.
