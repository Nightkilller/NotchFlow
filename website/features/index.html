Verified NotchFlow Features

A technical overview of the presentation and speech-synchronization capabilities built into NotchFlow for Apple Silicon MacBooks.

NotchFlow is built specifically to address the disconnection between digital presentation scripts and human audience engagement. Rather than forcing speakers to glance away from the camera lens to read a second monitor, physical cue cards, or an awkward desktop window, NotchFlow operates directly at the physical bezel level of modern MacBook displays.

1. Display Notch Alignment & Geometry Snapping

NotchFlow queries the active display safe-area insets via macOS AppKit APIs (NSScreen.safeAreaInsets). On modern MacBook Pro and MacBook Air models featuring a camera cutout, NotchFlow anchors its reading pod seamlessly beneath the notch, utilizing continuous corner curvatures that mirror the hardware display radius.

Practical Applications

  • Remote Keynotes & Webinars: Look directly into the audience without shifting your gaze downward.
  • External Monitor Support: On notchless displays or secondary screens, NotchFlow intelligently centers itself along the top bezel with smooth pill rounding.

2. Low-Latency Voice-Paced Synchronization

Unlike mechanical teleprompters that roll at an arbitrary words-per-minute speed, NotchFlow tracks your actual speech cadence. Leveraging Apple's on-device Speech framework (SFSpeechRecognizer), it aligns recognized phonemes and words against your pre-loaded script using a fuzzy matching sliding window.

Practical Applications

  • Ad-Libbing & Pauses: Drink water or answer a sudden question during a pitch; NotchFlow holds your place and resumes precisely when you speak the next sentence.
  • Variable Cadence: Speed up through familiar introductions or slow down during complex financial figures without touching a remote control.

3. High-Contrast Focus Word Highlighting

To prevent visual disorientation when tracking rapid paragraphs, NotchFlow separates the script into three distinct typographical focus states:

  • Completed Words: Softened opacity to diminish distractions from past text.
  • Active Word: High-contrast signature orange pill accent that guides the eye forward.
  • Upcoming Words: Clean high-contrast typography so you can anticipate sentence structures and inflection points.

4. Idle Auto-Shrink & Hover Expansion

Screen real estate is crucial during screen shares, live coding, and video calls. NotchFlow implements an intelligent pointer-tracking service. When your mouse leaves the prompter zone during a speech, NotchFlow smoothly animates into a streamlined, low-profile 2-line glance bar. Bringing your pointer near instantly restores full playback controls and script management.

5. Live Edge & Corner Resizing

Every speaker has different visual acuity and script preferences. NotchFlow supports direct live dragging across all window borders and corners. You can freely resize the reading canvas between 380 pt and 950 pt in width, and 180 pt to 650 pt in height. Dimension preferences are saved automatically across sessions.

6. Pure Dark & Studio Light Themes

With a single click or keyboard command, switch between a deep #000000 OLED black theme (perfect for discreet nighttime presentations and dark studio setups) and a crisp Studio White theme tailored for high-ambient daylight office spaces.

7. Multi-Format Document Import & Sanitization

Import preparation materials directly from Plain Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), or PDF documents. NotchFlow automatically strips extraneous markdown formatting tokens, headers, page numbers, and awkward line breaks, generating a clean linear reading stream ready for presentation.

8. Mirror Mode for Teleprompter Glass Rigs

When utilizing professional beam-splitter hardware rigs or 70/30 teleprompter glass in studio setups, NotchFlow includes horizontal reflection mirroring. This reverses the rendering pipeline along the vertical axis so text appears correct through reflection mirrors.

9. 100% On-Device Privacy & Offline Operation

Audio captured by NotchFlow is transcribed strictly on your local Apple Neural Engine and CPU cores. No audio streams, transcript fragments, or script contents are transmitted across networks or stored on external servers. You can run presentations in full airplane mode with complete confidence.
