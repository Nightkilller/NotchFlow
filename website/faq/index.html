Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers to common questions about NotchFlow compatibility, speech synchronization, installation, permissions, and security.

Compatibility & System Requirements

Does NotchFlow work on Macs without a physical camera notch?

Yes. While NotchFlow is geometrically tailored to hug the notch cutout of modern MacBook Pro and MacBook Air laptops, it works equally well on notchless Mac models (such as the 13-inch M1/M2 MacBook Pro, Mac mini, Mac Studio, or iMac) and external monitors. On notchless displays, NotchFlow cleanly anchors top-center with continuous rounded pill corners.

Which macOS versions and processors are supported?

NotchFlow requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma), macOS 15.0 (Sequoia), or later running on an Apple Silicon Mac (M1, M2, M3, M4). Intel-based Macs are not officially supported because voice-paced synchronization relies on Apple Silicon Neural Engine matrix coprocessors for sub-millisecond local speech evaluation.

Microphone & Privacy

Why does NotchFlow ask for Microphone permission?

NotchFlow uses your Mac's microphone strictly to listen to your voice and pace the teleprompter script to your natural speech. Speech recognition is handled locally by macOS's native SpeechKit framework.

Does my voice or script ever leave my computer?

Never. NotchFlow runs 100% offline. No audio streams, audio snippets, or script files are ever sent to external cloud servers, third-party analytics providers, or telemetry endpoints. You can run presentations with your Wi-Fi completely disconnected.

Features & Presentation Controls

What happens if I pause, cough, or ad-lib during my presentation?

NotchFlow's synchronization engine tracks your forward progress through the script using a forward confirmation window. If you pause, take a sip of water, or answer an audience question, the prompter holds your place. When you resume speaking words from your script, it automatically detects the match and continues scrolling seamlessly.

Can I resize the teleprompter window?

Yes. You can hover over any edge or corner of the NotchFlow window and drag to adjust its dimensions. The width can be adjusted from 380 pt to 950 pt, and the height from 180 pt to 650 pt. NotchFlow remembers your custom dimensions across application launches.

What is Idle Auto-Shrink mode?

When you move your pointer away from the prompter window while presenting, NotchFlow gently transitions into a compact 2-line glance bar so it does not obstruct your presentation slides, video feed, or notes. Moving your pointer back over the window instantly expands the full control interface.

What document formats can I import?

NotchFlow natively imports Plain Text (.txt), Markdown (.md), and PDF (.pdf) documents. During import, NotchFlow automatically cleans up formatting characters, headers, page numbers, and unnecessary line breaks.

Installation & Troubleshooting

How do I install NotchFlow?

You can install NotchFlow by downloading the official disk image (NotchFlow-Installer.dmg), opening it, and dragging the NotchFlow icon into your /Applications folder. You can also build from source or install via Homebrew once the cask is registered. See our full Installation Guide for step-by-step instructions.

What should I do if speech recognition stops following my voice?

First, verify that your active microphone is selected correctly in macOS System Settings > Sound > Input. Second, ensure that NotchFlow has microphone permission enabled in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone. For more steps, see our Troubleshooting Guide.
