What is NotchFlow?

NotchFlow is a native macOS teleprompter designed specifically for Apple Silicon MacBooks. It locks seamlessly under the display notch and tracks your voice in real time so you never break eye contact.

  • [*]
    Speech Synchronization Matches your exact speaking cadence word-for-word, scrolling smoothly and holding when you pause.
  • [*]
    MacBook Notch Integration Snaps directly underneath the camera notch for an unnoticeable, hardware-native appearance.
  • [*]
    100% On-Device & Offline Processes your voice entirely on your Mac's Neural Engine. No network requests, zero telemetry.
  • [*]
    Dynamic Edge Drag Resizing Hover near the borders to smoothly resize the teleprompter window from 380px to 950px.
  • [*]
    Dark & Light High-Contrast Modes Instant toggle with crystal clear typography that stays easily readable over any desktop background.
  • [*]
    Smart Auto-Shrink on Idle Collapses gracefully into a minimal status pill whenever speaking stops or your cursor moves away.

Privacy Guarantee

Your voice and presentation scripts never leave your machine. NotchFlow operates entirely offline using on-device Apple SpeechKit models. There are no accounts, no cloud endpoints, and no behavioral tracking.
